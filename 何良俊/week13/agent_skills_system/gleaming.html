gleaming

/ˈɡliːmɪŋ/
adj.闪闪发光的，明亮的

近义词

shining glowing glittering sparkling lustrous radiant

例句

  • The gleaming city lights reflected in the water.
    城市闪亮的灯光倒映在水中。
  • Her eyes were gleaming with excitement.
    她的眼中闪烁着兴奋的光芒。
  • The car's gleaming paintwork caught everyone's attention.
    汽车闪闪发光的漆面吸引了所有人的注意。
